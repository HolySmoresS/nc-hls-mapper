How to decide
  • Water: water covers most of the square. Plants or tree tops sticking out of the water still count as water.
  • Not water: ground, crops, grass, roads, roofs, or trees with dry ground under them.
  • Can't tell: thick trees hide the ground, too dark, blurry, or the square is on the grey edge of the photo.
  • Look at the square only, not the rest of the photo. When unsure, choose Can't tell.
